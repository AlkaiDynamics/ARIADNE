Status: `QUARANTINED_POST_GATE`

**Śrī Yantra remains outside KEYSTONE execution.**

- not a training or example domain for KEYSTONE;
- not admissible evidence for `K`;
- not eligible for real-domain detector execution until both Phase-1 KEYSTONE and the blind-domain protocol are frozen.

```text
PHASE1_KEYSTONE_FROZEN
AND
BLIND_DOMAIN_PROTOCOL_FROZEN
```
