# AYLI → SUN/SATURN → Rosetta → ARIADNE
## Research Timeline and Thread Genealogy

### Purpose

This document is a **machine-readable continuity seed for ARIADNE**.

Its job is not to prove the historical, mathematical, religious, or scientific claims discussed in the research. Its job is to preserve:

1. **what question we were asking at each stage;**
2. **what changed our model;**
3. **which claims were promoted, demoted, split, corrected, or abandoned;**
4. **how later threads grew from earlier threads;**
5. **which unresolved threads remain live;**
6. **why the current ARIADNE architecture exists.**

The most important thing for ARIADNE to understand is that this project did **not** progress by following one uninterrupted evidentiary chain. Its productive method repeatedly alternated between local source work, global structural comparison, cross-era and cross-language jumps, different classes occupying similar structural addresses, negative controls and failed correspondences, and returns to old unresolved threads after new evidence changed their meaning.

## Reading rules

- `CHAT-DIRECT`: directly recovered conversation transcript or dated chat export.
- `CHAT-RECONSTRUCTED`: later reconstruction where the full raw chat boundary is not yet recovered.
- `ARTIFACT-MILESTONE`: dated file, theorem, charter, ledger, spreadsheet, or repository state.
- `CURRENT-CHAT`: material from the active September 13 conversation.
- `PROJECT-PREHISTORY`: earlier work that materially shaped AYLI.

A conversational assertion is **not** source-explicit historical evidence merely because it appears here. Earlier wrong or incomplete models remain preserved as `prior_state → correction_event → current_state`. Dependent claims do not become independent confirmation. Failed mappings and nulls remain records.

The recurring research principle became:

`find matches → preserve failures → cluster failures → find missing variables → extend the model → retest`

Working phrase: **Truth through failure towards alignment.**
